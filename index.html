Hesper Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Hesper is made by Peralabs, Istanbul, Türkiye · peralabs.dev@gmail.com

Hesper is a private journal you speak to. It writes your words down and remembers your life with you: the people, the promises, the stories. This policy says what that takes, where your data goes, and how to delete it. In short: your journal is used only to give it back to you. It isn't sold, it isn't used for ads, and Hesper has no tracking or analytics.

Hesper never asks for your contacts, location, or health data, and doesn't analyse your photos: they're kept as part of your journal and nothing more.

AI providers may keep requests for a limited time (up to 30 days) to prevent abuse, as their API terms allow. Some of these companies are in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual protections.

Deleting

  • A moment: deleting it also deletes its photos, its transcript and everything learned from it. Any insight that depended on it is withdrawn.
  • Your account: in the app, Life › You › Delete account. Everything is deleted: your journal, photos, people, promises, stories, insights and questions, and your subscription record at RevenueCat. If you signed in with Apple, Apple is told to disconnect Hesper. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they're overwritten.
  • Your subscription is with Apple: deleting your account doesn't cancel it. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your data in the app at any time; your corrections are kept. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any question about it, at peralabs.dev@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU or Türkiye) you have further rights, including to object and to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Your data travels encrypted. Each account can only reach its own rows and files, enforced by the database itself, and files are kept in private storage.

Children

Hesper isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it applies.
